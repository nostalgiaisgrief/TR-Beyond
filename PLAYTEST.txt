TOMB RECONSTRUCTION - GYM PLAYTEST 06

Run Play.cmd. Keep the build and work folders together. No DOSBox, CD mount,
Python or compiler is needed to play this Windows build. Source and build tools
remain in the main tomb-reconstruction project.

CHANGES IN 06
Projection now matches the DOS 80-degree horizontal FOV and near/far planes.
Immediate camera cuts remain immediate.
Chase, swimming and fixed-shot camera positions follow reconstructed DOS
routines at 30 Hz: original animation-bound focus, pitch, distance, sector/box
clearance, integer smoothing and vertical correction. Rendering interpolates
between these camera states. Custom wall/doorway recovery and framing have
been removed. Debug arrow-key orbit/zoom remains optional for testing.
Earlier audio, movement and graphics fixes are included.

CONTROLS
W run; S hop backward; A/D turn; Shift+W/S walk.
J or Alt jump; Ctrl action / catch / hold a ledge.
Underwater: J/Alt swim, W/S pitch down/up, A/D turn.
Surface: W/S forward/back, Q/E sideways, J/Alt dive, Ctrl climb out at an edge.
Arrow keys temporarily orbit / zoom; C recenter.
P pause; R restart the gym; Esc close. F6 skips to the pool for testing.

FULL-ROUTE ACCEPTANCE PLAYTEST
1. Start normally and follow the spoken gym instructions. Listen for missing,
   repeated, interrupted or incorrectly timed prompts.
2. Complete the movement, jump and climbing exercises. Check landings on the
   upper platform, normal/handstand pull-ups, hanging and sideways movement.
3. Check windows, pillars and doorways. Look for unrelated geometry, disappearing
   connected rooms, a stuck camera or repeated camera zooming near walls.
4. Enter the pool, pitch up/down, swim, surface, dive and climb out. Finish the
   pool exercise and wait for the title to show Gym complete. Press R and verify
   the starting position, health and tutorial are reset.
5. Fatal falls are covered by automation; no special gym fall is required.
   Drowning can be checked in the pool. R should restart cleanly. Space/focus loss
   should pause speech and effects; reset/close should stop both.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
This is a development playtest, not a finished full-game replacement. Swan dives
from land, sliding, general object interactions, object/environment sound effects outside the gym, inventory,
saves, menus, combat and FMVs are not implemented. Avoid Shift during a forward
jump: that requests the unsupported swan dive. Other unsupported behaviour is
reported in the window title. Rendering, lighting and some camera details differ
from DOS. No manual full-route acceptance pass is claimed yet.

For a bug report, include a screenshot with the window title, the exercise or
location, which keys were held, and whether it also happens after R/reset.
